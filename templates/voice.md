# voice.md

Copy this to `~/.claude/tiktok/voice.md` and fill it in. Every skill in the
pack reads it. If you would rather not fill it in by hand, give Claude the
transcripts or captions of three of your own TikToks and say "write my
voice.md from these".

On TikTok you say most of your words out loud, so a line you would never say
is a retake, not a draft.
